About The Property

A Regal Sanctuary of Mewar Heritage & Hospitality

The Kushal Bagh Palace is a magnificent heritage property located in Savina, Udaipur, Rajasthan. Built as an emblem of regal splendor, the property offers guests an authentic taste of royal Rajputana living combined with warm, personalized hospitality.

Featuring 20 exquisitely appointed rooms, expansive lush green gardens, a crystal-clear swimming pool, an AC restaurant serving royal culinary delights, and an open café area for serene evenings, The Kushal Bagh Palace stands as Udaipur's premier destination for luxury heritage stays, family vacations, and memorable celebrations.
