About The Property

Wildlife Camping & Adventure Stay in Jawai

Namo Adventure Camp is located in the wilderness of Jawai, Rajasthan — renowned for its majestic granite hills, natural caves, and rare human-leopard harmony. It is a dream destination for wildlife photography, nature treks, and thrilling adventures.

Offering 12 total accommodations (06 Deluxe Rooms and 06 Super Deluxe AC Camps), Namo Adventure Camp features a Big Garden, a multi-cuisine Restaurant, an Open Café Area, Adventure Activities, Wildlife Experiences, Nature Stays, Luxury Camping, and a family-friendly environment.
