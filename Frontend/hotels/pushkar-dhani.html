About The Property

Authentic Heritage Camping in Sacred Pushkar

Pushkar Dhani is a premier luxury camping and resort destination located in the sacred town of Pushkar, Rajasthan. Surrounded by desert landscape and rich culture, the property captures the soul of traditional Rajasthani village life while offering guests unmatched modern comforts.

Offering 06 Deluxe Rooms and 06 Super Deluxe Room AC Camps, Pushkar Dhani provides a unique blend of heritage hospitality, Big Garden, Swimming Pool, multi-cuisine Restaurant, and Open Café Area — perfect for luxury camping experiences, family holidays, and cultural getaways.
