About The Property

Luxury Thar Desert Camping in Sam, Jaisalmer

Namo Desert Camp Talai offers an extraordinary Thar Desert luxury experience situated amidst the legendary Sam Sand Dunes of Jaisalmer, Rajasthan. Designed to immerse guests in authentic desert heritage with world-class hospitality, it is the ultimate destination for desert lovers.

Featuring 23 accommodations (13 Deluxe Room Camps, 06 Super Deluxe AC Camps, and 04 AC Hut Cottages), a Big Garden, an AC Restaurant, an Open Café Area, exciting Camel Safaris, evening Bonfires, and traditional Rajasthani Cultural Programs, Namo Desert Camp Talai provides a memorable family-friendly desert stay under the stars.
